About this Website

This project was born out of a desire to support emotional wellbeing and help others feel more connected to God, especially during the ups and downs of everyday life. Created as a personal initiative and in support of the 'Dóchas' Christian Society at the University of Galway, this website offers a simple yet meaningful way to engage with Scripture in moments when you need it most.

Whether you're feeling joyful, anxious, lonely, thankful, or anything in between, this tool lets you choose your current emotion and receive a Bible verse chosen to speak directly to that feeling. The goal is to provide encouragement, comfort, and spiritual reflection tailored to where you are emotionally.

This platform is more than just a verse generator—it's a space for personal connection, faith exploration, and emotional care. Whether you're deepening your spiritual life, seeking peace during a tough time, or simply looking for a bit of encouragement during your day, I hope this site brings you closer to God and reminds you that you're never alone.


How does it works?

Simply choose an emotion from a list, and the site will provide a Bible verse that aligns with how you're feeling. These verses are thoughtfully selected to offer encouragement, wisdom, and peace.

While this tool can offer encouragement, it’s not a substitute for personal devotion, prayer, or pastoral care. It’s meant to complement your faith journey, not replace it.


Mobile/Accessibility Friendly

This website is built with simplicity, clarity, and inclusivity in mind. Whether you're checking in on your phone between classes, taking a quiet break at home, or browsing late at night, the experience is designed to be smooth and accessible across all screen sizes. The layout automatically adapts to mobile devices, ensuring that text remains readable, buttons are easy to tap, and navigation is effortless. Even when you're on the go, you can quickly find a verse that speaks to your heart.

Accessibility is also a key priority. The site follows best practices for visual contrast, keyboard navigation, screen reader compatibility, and responsive design. Everyone—regardless of physical ability or device—should be able to engage with Scripture in a way that feels personal and seamless.

As the project grows, I hope to continue improving accessibility features, so that emotional and spiritual care can truly be available to all.


Future Plans

In the future, I hope to add features like saving your favorite verses, daily encouragement, or reflections to help deepen your faith walk. For now, I hope this simple tool serves as a quiet companion in your spiritual journey.

While this site will begin as a Christian-focused tool, rooted in Scripture and created for the 'Dóchas, Christian Society at the University of Galway, I believe that emotional wellbeing and spiritual reflection are important for everyone—regardless of background or belief.

In the future, I hope to expand this project to include other faith traditions, such as Islam and Judaism, by offering verses or reflections from the Qur’an, Torah, and other sacred texts. The goal is to create an emotionally supportive and spiritually enriching space for a wider community—while honoring the uniqueness of each tradition.

Each version of the site will be handled with care, collaboration, and respect, with the help of knowledgeable contributors from each faith, to ensure that verses are presented in a way that is both theologically sound and emotionally relevant.
