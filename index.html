This site was created to help you feel closer to God by offering Bible verses that speak to your current emotions. Whether you're feeling overwhelmed, grateful, lost, or hopeful, simply select how you're feeling and receive a verse chosen to encourage and uplift you.

Made with care for the Christian Society at my university, this is a space for spiritual reflection, emotional support, and connection with God—one verse at a time.
